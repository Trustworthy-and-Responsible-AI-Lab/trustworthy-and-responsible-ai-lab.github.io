What We Look For

Motivation comes first. We value curiosity, persistence, and a genuine drive to solve hard problems. Ideal candidates have a strong background in computer security, machine learning, or both. Before reaching out, we encourage you to browse our course materials and recent publications to gauge your interest and preparedness.
